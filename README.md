# ♟️ ChessCoach AI - Unlimited Free Chess.com Game Review

An authentic, 1:1 replica of **Chess.com's Android Game Review and Personal Chess Coach**, built so you can analyze unlimited games for free without paying for a Diamond subscription.

---

## 📸 Matched Directly to Chess.com Android App

Based directly on the official app interface:
1. **Coach Speech Bubble Above the Board**:
   - Features the smiling coach avatar with glasses and collared blazer.
   - White rounded speech bubble with evaluation pill (e.g. `+0.30`, `+0.26`, `-0.16`).
   - Move badge and title: `📖 2...Nc6 is a book move`, `❌ Bd3 is a miss`, `? e5 is a mistake`.
   - Concise, natural language coaching explanation.
   - Future continuation line display (e.g. `12... ♛xd3 13. cxd3`).

2. **Future Analysis & Continuation Engine**:
   - Tap **Show**: The board advances and shows the future punishment/continuation line calculated by the engine!
   - Tap **Best**: Instantly displays the best move on the board with green arrow.
   - Tap **Hide** or **Resume**: Snaps back to the game position.

3. **Horizontal Move Strip (Under Board)**:
   - Displays moves horizontally with piece glyphs (`2. ♘c3  2...♘c6  3. ♘f3  e6  4. d4`).
   - Active move is underlined with a clean white indicator.
   - Blunders and mistakes display their badge icon right in the ribbon.

4. **Action Bottom Bar**:
   - `Show` / `Hide`: Toggle future continuation lines.
   - `Best`: Show the best move alternative.
   - `Retry`: Interactive tactical challenge mode to find the best move yourself on the board.
   - **Next** / **Resume**: Big vibrant green action button.

5. **Exact Move Badges & CAPS Accuracy**:
   - 💎 **Brilliant (!!)**, 🔷 **Great (!) , ⭐ **Best**, 🟢 **Excellent**, 🔵 **Good**, 📖 **Book**, 🟡 **Inaccuracy (?!)**, 🟠 **Mistake (?)**, 🔴 **Blunder (??)**, ❌ **Miss**.
   - Full White and Black Accuracy % (CAPS) and Estimated Elo ratings.

6. **Free AI Integration & Engine Accuracy**:
   - Raw chess truth (evaluations, centipawns, PV lines) calculated 100% by the engine.
   - Rich, conversational coaching text generated with zero rate limits, plus optional free Gemini API key support in Settings (`⚙️`).

## 🚀 How to Run

1. Open [`index.html`](file:///c:/Users/ROG/Desktop/ChessAi/index.html) in your browser (Chrome, Edge, Brave).
2. To load your own games:
   - Tap the `⚙️` Settings gear or `←` in the top header.
   - Select your `.pgn` file or paste your moves.
   - Tap **"Start AI Game Review"**!
3. To install on Android:
   - Open the page in Chrome on Android.
   - Tap `⋮` -> **"Add to Home screen"** to install it as a native standalone app!
